# Class Guidelines and Structure

## Part 1: Breaks in the Class

- **One-hour class**
  - Nobody will be allowed to leave the class.

- **Two-hour class**
  - After one hour:
    - 4 students at a time will be allowed to go out of the class for **3–4 minutes** break.
    - At this time, you have to submit your **ID card** to me (**Mandatory**).
    - If you do not come within the given time period, I will have to note down your **registration number**, and you can take back your ID card.

- **Three-hour class**
  - We will take a break of **07 minutes** for refreshment after 2 hours.

## Part 3: Code on Laptop

- DSA is not just about coding syntax, loops, and functions.
- It is about your **logical thinking**.
- So, in class, we will **not code directly on the laptop**.

### Steps to Solve Problems

1. **Your laptop should be into your bag** (**Mandatory**).
2. For the given problem, take your copy and pen:
   - Write **pseudocode** for the given problems (**For all problems**).
   - Dry run all the given problems.
   - Verbal solutions will **not** be accepted.
3. Inform me that you have completed this part.
4. Now you can code given problems on your laptop.
